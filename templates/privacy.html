Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

This website is a private wedding-planning site operated by {{ couple }} (“we,” “us,” or “our”) for personal use by the couple and a small group of people helping plan our wedding. This policy explains what information the site handles and how we use it. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information we handle

  • Account information. Planners sign in with a username and password so that planning pages stay private.
  • Guest and event details that we enter ourselves to plan the wedding (such as names, RSVP responses, schedule, budget, and vendor notes).
  • Content you add to the planning tools, including notes, to-dos, and images you upload or import to the idea board.

Pinterest integration

Our idea board offers an optional connection to Pinterest so we can gather wedding inspiration in one place. If you choose to connect a Pinterest account:

  • We request read-only access to your boards and pins (the boards:read and pins:read permissions only).
  • We use that access solely to import images and their descriptions from a board you select into a private idea board on this site.
  • We never post, edit, follow, message, or change anything on your Pinterest account, and we do not access boards or pins you have not asked us to import.
  • Imported images are stored on our own server so the idea board keeps working even if a pin later changes. We do not share Pinterest data with any third party.
  • You can revoke our access at any time from your Pinterest account under Settings → Security and permissions → Apps. Revoking access stops any future imports.

How we use information

Information is used only to operate the planning tools on this site — for example, to display the guest list, track the budget, and show the idea board. We do not sell, rent, or trade information, and we do not use it for marketing.

Sharing and third-party services

We use a small number of services to run the site: Pinterest (for the optional import described above), Google Sheets (so a planner can read an export of our own planning data), and an email provider (to send wedding correspondence). These services only receive the data needed to provide their function, and each is governed by its own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

We keep planning data for as long as it is useful for the wedding and then delete it. You can ask us to delete data connected to you — including any content imported from Pinterest — by emailing us at the address below, and we will remove it.

Cookies

The site uses a single session cookie to keep planners signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Children

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent change.
